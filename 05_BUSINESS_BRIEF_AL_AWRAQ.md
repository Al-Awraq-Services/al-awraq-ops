## Working Assumption
This business appears to be a service-operation company whose website should primarily generate leads and support operational follow-through rather than just display marketing content.

## Likely Business Problems to Validate
- Leads may arrive through phone, WhatsApp, form, or referrals with no central tracking.
- Quotes may be handled manually.
- Technician scheduling may be informal.
- Job statuses may not be visible in one dashboard.
- Repeat customer history may be fragmented.
- Follow-up, reminders, and invoicing may be inconsistent.

## Questions That Must Be Answered Before Product Design
- What exact services are offered today?
- Which services need bookings versus inspections versus quotations?
- Is pricing fixed, variable, area-based, tonnage-based, labor-based, or custom quoted?
- Who answers leads?
- Who creates quotations?
- Who schedules technicians?
- How many technicians or field staff exist?
- Is the service area only Dubai or wider UAE?
- Are jobs residential, commercial, or both?
- Is there recurring maintenance contract logic?
- Is payment collected before, during, or after service?
- Is there invoicing?
- Is WhatsApp central to the workflow?
- What data must be captured per customer and per job?
- Does the owner need reporting on revenue, leads, staff productivity, repeat customers, and job completion?

## Likely App Modules to Explore
- Lead capture and inquiry management
- Quotation management
- Booking calendar
- Technician assignment
- Job status tracking
- Customer CRM
- Invoice / payment status tracking
- WhatsApp-triggered communication
- Admin dashboard and reporting
- File/photo upload for before/after job evidence

## Non-Negotiable Note
No serious app specification should be written from public website information alone. Internal workflow facts are mandatory.
