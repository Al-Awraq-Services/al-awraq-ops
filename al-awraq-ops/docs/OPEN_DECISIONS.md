## Standing rule — verification credential policy (added after Phase C4 review; not an open decision, informational)
Changing a privileged account's live credential (as happened to the Admin password during C4 verification) is a **controlled exception**, not standard test procedure, and must not be repeated casually in future phases. Preferred future verification path, in order: (1) a known test credential already documented/held by the developer; (2) an already-authenticated active session; (3) pre-created, non-owner test accounts (`Test Staff`, `Test Technician`) where the scope under test doesn't require Admin privileges. A reset-token-based credential change is a last resort, used only when none of those are available, and must be disclosed with the same rigor as the C4 entry every time it happens. Full policy text in `docs/SECURITY_BASELINE.md` ("Verification Credential Policy"). **Followed successfully in Phase C5**: the same working session's C4 credentials were reused, and no credential was reset.

## Resolved by developer authorization, two earlier decisions superseded (Invoicing pass — 2026-09-13)
- The developer asked for invoicing to trigger "for every job done, or/and a quotation approved," viewable via a button beside the Job description. **Job does not exist in this codebase** (still Phase D2/D3, listed as unbuilt everywhere this file and `HANDOVER_LOG.md` mention it) — told directly before any scoping began. The developer chose to skip Job for this pass and confirmed "automatically generated" means a real automatic trigger, not a separate generate button.
- Two earlier decisions were superseded, both developer-directed here, not sponsor-approved, and both disclosed rather than silently changed:
  1. **Quote lifecycle** was sponsor-approved (Phase 3/5) as "DRAFT, SENT only." It now has a third status, `APPROVED`, which auto-generates an Invoice. If the sponsor's eventual answer on Quote lifecycle differs, reverting is not free — Invoices already generated from Approved quotes exist as real rows by then, and removing the status would need a real decision on what happens to them, not just a schema rollback.
  2. **Quote's "no lock at any status" precedent** (established C3 for Lead, extended to Quote in D1) no longer holds once a Quote reaches Approved — it becomes read-only, the same way an archived Customer or a deactivated Service already is. Reasoning: an Invoice is a copy of the Quote's line items at approval time; letting the Quote keep changing afterward would let that copy silently stop matching its source.
- Built at `/invoices` + `/invoices/[invoiceId]`, triggered from the Quote detail page — see `HANDOVER_LOG.md`/`CHANGE_LOG.md` for full technical detail (schema, the approval transaction, the `quoteId @unique` database-level double-invoice guard).
- **Explicitly deferred to a follow-up pass, per the developer's own "function first, fine-tune later" framing** — none of these are oversights, all are open for the next pass:
  - No "mark unpaid" to undo a mistaken "Mark as paid" click.
  - No partial payments — an Invoice is binary Unpaid/Paid, no tracking of a partial amount received.
  - No PDF or printable/emailable export — an Invoice is only viewable as an internal app screen right now, not a document that can leave the app.
  - No notification to anyone when an Invoice is generated or paid.
  - No Job-completion trigger, since Job doesn't exist — when it's eventually built, the invoicing design (one Invoice, generated server-side, snapshotted line items) should extend to it without a redesign, but that extension itself is not built.
  - No customer-facing invoice view — invoices are Admin+Staff only, same as Quote; a customer cannot see their own invoice anywhere in this app.
